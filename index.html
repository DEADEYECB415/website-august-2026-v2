Finding us

There's no sign
on the door

We're tucked inside the brick building with the large arched windows across the street from the City Hall steps. No storefront, no awning, no reason to notice us from the sidewalk. Walk in anyway — we're the first thing on your left.

Item highlights

Dead Eye

Drip coffee. Three shots of espresso. The drink we took our name from.

Tahitian Vanilla

Our own vanilla syrup. Velvet on the finish.

Banana Cream Pie

The one people drive over for. Dessert in a cup, unapologetically.

Banana Matcha Cream Pie

Same pie, built on house premium matcha instead of espresso.

Guava Matcha

House premium matcha — smooth, balanced with delicate nutty notes topped with a vibrant guava cream.

Ube

Purple yam from the Philippines. Sweet, nutty, faintly like vanilla. Add a shot or don't.

Ferrero Rocher

A smooth and creamy hazelnut latte, topped with a velvety chocolate cream and finished with crushed toffee crumble.

Pandan

House-made pandan syrup. Somewhere between vanilla, almond and jasmine.
